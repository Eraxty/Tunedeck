TuneDeck

Send your music library to the TV.

Import a library export

  1. Spotify: spotify.com → Account → Privacy settings → Download your data (Account data). Spotify emails a zip in a few days.
  2. YouTube / YouTube Music: takeout.google.com → select only YouTube and YouTube Music → export.

Upload the .zip as it arrives, or individual .json / .csv files. Re-upload a newer export any time to sync.

Connect Spotify (live sync)

Start Connect Spotify in the TV's Settings first, then tap below. Your browser will warn that the TV's connection is not private; that's the TV's own certificate, choose to continue.
